# Visual DNA

## Metaphor
The page is a river carrying artifacts of the Chinese internet.

## Motion
- Water first, archive second, revival third.
- Movement is slow, viscous, archaeological.
- Pointer interaction is disturbance, not control.
- Scroll is time.

## Surfaces
- almost-black water
- ink gradients
- stone / paper / oxidized metal-inspired texture
- restrained warm white typography
- dark gold only for recovered/revived states

## Avoid
- cyberpunk
- neon
- generic glassmorphism
- SaaS pricing cards
- dashboard grids
- floating 3D blobs with no meaning
- excessive glow
